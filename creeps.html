Creeps and waves

Every wave in the game: who comes, how tough they are, how fast they move, what they resist and what they pay. Numbers are read from the game itself.

Damage against armour

Each gem's damage is multiplied by the creep's armour type, then reduced by its armour value: each point of armour cuts damage by about 6% (5 armour takes 23% off, 15 takes 47%). Pure damage ignores both, and magic damage (burn, poison, Wings of Topaz) ignores armour but is cut by magic resistance.

Waves 1–50

Ten creeps a wave, except every tenth wave, which is a single boss. Speed is in tiles per second. Gold is per creep (or for the boss); a wave with no leaks also pays a bonus of 5 + the wave number.
