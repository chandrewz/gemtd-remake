Gem TD Remastered

Fifty waves, then endless. One castle. Build the maze out of the gems you are dealt.

  1. Each round you place five random gems. Every gem blocks the path, so place them to build a maze.
  2. Keep one gem (or keep it downgraded one quality). The other four turn into rocks that still block. Don't like your five? Pay gold to lift them off and place five new ones. Click a rock and press R to clear it.
  3. Two identical gems combine one quality higher. Four identical gems combine two qualities higher.
  4. Gems that match a recipe combine into special towers. Open Towers to plan.
  5. Towers rank up every 10 kills for extra damage. Combining keeps the kills.
  6. Creeps walk Portal → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → Castle by the shortest route. Flyers ignore the maze.
